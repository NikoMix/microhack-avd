**Contoso Ltd.** runs **~1,000 users** on an ageing on-premises VDI estate — a mix of
**Microsoft RDS** and **Citrix Virtual Apps & Desktops** across two datacentres. Hardware
refresh is due, the Citrix licensing renewal is looming, and remote-work demand has outgrown
the gateway. Contoso wants to **migrate to Azure Virtual Desktop** to cut cost, simplify
operations, and improve the remote experience — **without a big-bang cutover**.

<Aside type="note" title="One source of truth">
  All scenario numbers are **fictitious** and live in
  [`scenario/`](https://github.com/NikoMix/microhack-avd/tree/main/scenario). Use these figures
  everywhere; do not invent different ones unless a challenge explicitly asks for a variation.
</Aside>

## The four personas

| Persona | Profile | Users | Topology |
|---|---|---|---|
| Knowledge workers | Pooled multi-session | **700** | Win 11 Enterprise multi-session |
| Call centre | Pooled multi-session, time-based scaling | **200** | High density, predictable hours |
| Power users | Personal | **60** | Persistent, heavier apps |
| Developers | Personal | **40** | Custom installs, premium disk |
| **Total** | | **1000** | |

The split — **700 + 200 + 60 + 40 = 1,000** — must reconcile in every artifact you produce.

<Aside type="caution" title="Paper design vs. lab slice">
  The full 1,000-user footprint is a **paper design**. In the lab you deploy a **token slice**
  (1–2 session hosts, small SKUs). Guardrails and teardown live in
  [`scripts/`](https://github.com/NikoMix/microhack-avd/tree/main/scripts) and the
  `avd-cost-guardrails` skill.
</Aside>
