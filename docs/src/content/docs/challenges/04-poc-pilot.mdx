## Objective

Design and evaluate the AVD **Proof-of-Concept / Pilot**: **scope** (in/out), **quantitative
success criteria**, a **test plan**, **results capture**, and a **go/no-go** recommendation that
de-risks the production rollout.

## The agentic way (do this first)

- **Agent:** `avd-pilot-lead`.
- **Skills:** `avd-evidence-pack` (the `pilot.md` template), `avd-personas-sizing` (set realistic
  density/perf targets).
- **MCP:** `azure` to observe the pilot environment; `github` to track scope and results.

## Evidence artifact

`pilot.md` — scope in/out, measurable success criteria, the test plan, results captured against
the criteria, and a go/no-go recommendation.

## Definition of done

- **Scope** is explicit: which personas, apps, and regions are **in** and **out**.
- Success criteria are **quantitative** (e.g. logon time, app-launch time, density, profile load).
- A **test plan** maps each criterion to a repeatable test.
- Results are captured **against** the criteria, and a clear **go/no-go** is recommended.

<Aside type="caution" title="Criteria before tests">
  Agree the numeric thresholds with the customer **before** you test, or the pilot becomes an
  opinion. The thresholds carry straight into Challenge 06.
</Aside>
