## Objective

Assemble the AVD **operational handover** — a **Low-Level Design** (as-built), an **operations
runbook**, a **knowledge-transfer plan**, a **hypercare plan**, a **customer sign-off**, and
**lessons learned** — turning the engagement's evidence into a coherent pack the customer's team
can run.

## The agentic way (do this first)

- **Agent:** `avd-handover-writer`.
- **Skills:** `avd-evidence-pack` (handover templates), `avd-landing-zone`, `avd-fslogix`.
- **MCP:** `azure` to confirm the **as-built** state; `github` to assemble the artifacts.

## Evidence artifacts

A **Low-Level Design**, an **operations runbook**, a **KT plan**, a **hypercare plan**, a
**customer sign-off**, and a **lessons-learned** record.

## Definition of done

- The **LLD reflects what was actually deployed** (as-built, reconciled with C05/C06).
- The **runbook** covers common ops tasks (scaling, image updates, profile issues, onboarding).
- A **KT plan** and a **hypercare plan** (with exit criteria) are in place.
- A **customer sign-off** records acceptance; **lessons learned** are captured for next time.

<Aside type="note" title="Close the loop">
  The handover is where the engagement becomes auditable as a whole — every prior artifact should
  be referenced and reconciled here.
</Aside>
